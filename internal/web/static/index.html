Why this exists

SCDNR tags Atlantic blue crabs around Charleston and depends on crabbers to report the ones they catch. By SCDNR's own account fewer than half of the tags anglers encounter are ever reported, and the fisheries literature puts much of the blame on how rewards are delivered: the finder's identity gets lost, weeks pass, and by the time anything arrives the incentive is gone.

Here the reward is locked to the tag itself. Scanning it pays the finder in seconds, and their position and measurements are written into the same transaction that moves the money, so the reward and the data cannot be separated afterwards.

What the chain does and doesn't prove

Time and integrity
Every tagging and every report is a Bitcoin transaction. Once mined, the record cannot be backdated or edited by anyone, including SCDNR.
Possession of the tag
A reward can only be unlocked with the key printed on the tag, so a report can only have come from someone holding it.
Paid exactly once
Each reward is a single coin. A duplicate claim is a double-spend and fails.
Location
Positions are reported by the phone that scanned the tag and are taken on trust. Reported accuracy is recorded alongside every fix. This is an attestation, not proof of where anyone stood.
Release
Whether an animal really went back in the water cannot be shown on chain. The bonus for releasing one is held and paid only if the tag is reported again, which is corroboration rather than proof.
