What this record does and doesn't prove

The time
Your report is written into a Bitcoin transaction. Once a block carries it, the record cannot be backdated or altered: not by you, not by SCDNR, not by us.
That you had the tag
The reward can only be unlocked with the key printed on the tag itself, so the record can only have come from someone holding it.
That it pays once
The reward is a single coin. A second claim on the same tag is a double-spend and simply fails.
Where you were
The position comes from this phone and is taken on trust. We record how accurate the phone claims to be, and we tie the fix to your report so it cannot be edited afterwards, but none of that establishes where anyone actually stood, and we will not pretend otherwise.
That you released it
Also taken on trust. The bonus for putting an animal back is held rather than paid, and released to you only if this tag is reported again, which is the closest thing to evidence a release can have.
